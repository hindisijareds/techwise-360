Lab Headset Station Pairing

Pair your active dashboard session directly to the physical Meta Quest 2 at your computer lab desk. Your student account, grade level, and active quarter are automatically synchronized to the headset without typing usernames or passwords in VR.

How Station Pairing Works:

  1. Locate Station: Check the station label on your desk or headset (e.g., Station 01).
  2. Click Pair: Select your station above and click Pair & Launch on Headset.
  3. Put On Headset: Put on the Meta Quest 2 headset. The VR simulation automatically recognizes your account, displays your name, and unlocks your learning modules!
